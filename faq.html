Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Contact us directly.

Shipping
How long does delivery take?

Most orders ship within 2–3 business days and arrive within 5–7 days depending on your location. You'll get updates by SMS (and email, if provided) as your order moves.

Is shipping free?

Yes, on orders above ₹2,500. Below that, a flat shipping fee applies at checkout — you'll see the exact amount before you pay.

Can I track my order?

Yes — use Track Order with your Order ID and the phone number used at checkout.

Sizing
How do I pick the right size?

Each product page has a Size Guide link with detailed measurements. Since pieces are handmade in small batches, we recommend checking the guide even if you know your usual size elsewhere.

What if it doesn't fit?

Reach out within 3 days of delivery via Contact Us with your Order ID, and we'll help sort out an exchange where possible.

Payments
What payment methods do you accept?

UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or any UPI app) and direct Net Banking transfer. We don't use a payment gateway, so you confirm payment on-screen after paying, and we verify it manually before shipping.

Is it safe to pay this way?

You're paying directly to our verified UPI ID or bank account — the same as paying any local shop. We never ask for OTPs, PINs, or card details, and never will.

Account & Orders
Do I need an account to order?

No — you can check out as a guest. Creating an account just lets you track order history and save your cart across devices.

I forgot my password.

Password recovery isn't available yet — for now, please contact us with your registered phone number and we'll help you regain access.
